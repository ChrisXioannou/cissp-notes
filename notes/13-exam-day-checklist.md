# Exam Day Checklist

1.  Read the **actor** first. It sets the vocabulary.

2.  Underline the **qualifier**. FIRST, BEST, MOST, PRIMARY, GREATEST, NOT, EXCEPT, current, documented, interim, periodically.

3.  Pick the **ladder**: sequence for FIRST/NEXT, tier for BEST/MOST, impact for GREATEST RISK, membership for NOT/EXCEPT.

4.  **Tag every option**: CIA leg, control type, tier.

5.  Apply the **eliminators**: absolute language, two options meaning the same thing, broad generic answers, overly technical answers.

6.  **Count the requirements** in the stem and the coverage per option.

7.  Read the **governance option first**, not last.

**Ask: what would a CISO who has never touched a terminal choose here?**

Recurring traps to watch for:

- Reaching for the technical or detective control when a governance or preventive answer exists

- Acting before assessing on FIRST questions where nothing is burning

- Unfamiliar ordinary-English vocabulary

- Reading past the qualifier

- Picking the more effortful or impressive-sounding answer
